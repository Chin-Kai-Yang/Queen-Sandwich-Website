Our First Store

In 1987

We opened our first outlet in

Miami, Florida

At Sandwich Queen, we believe that food should be more than just fuel; it should be an experience of flavor, freshness, and nourishment. From day one, our mission has been to create sandwiches that bring people together and inspire them to make healthier, more conscious choices. We start with the best ingredients - fresh, organic, and locally sourced - because we know quality is the foundation of every good meal. But it's not just about ingredients; it's about the care, dedication, and passion we put into every sandwich we make. We're here to make a difference, one sandwich at a time, and we hope that every bite reminds our customers that simple choices can have a lasting impact on our health, our community, and our planet. Our goal is to keep pushing boundaries, to never compromise on quality, and to create a place where people know they're not just customers - they're family
