Sandwich Queen's FAQs

1. What are your opening hours?

Our store is open daily from '7.00am~10.30pm' for both dine-in and online orders. Hours may vary by location; please check our website or app for the hours of your local restaurant.

2. Do I need to make a reservation?

No reservations are needed for our fast food restaurant. Feel free to walk in or order online for pickup or delivery.

3. How do I make a reservation?

Since we're a fast-casual restaurant, reservations aren't required. Simply order ahead or stop by at your convenience.

4. How do I see my orders record?

You can view your order history by logging into your account and selecting "Order History." Here, you'll see past orders, tracking information, and receipts.

5. What is the difference of local shop & our shop?

While local shops may offer similar items, our sandwiches are made with a unique blend of fresh ingredients and exclusive recipes. We also offer online ordering, customization options, and loyalty rewards exclusive to our brand.

6. Do you offer any vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free option?

Yes, we have a selection of vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Our online menu has a filter feature to help you find these items easily.

7. Do you have kid-friendly menu options?

Yes, we offer a kids' menu with smaller, kid-friendly sandwich options, sides, and drinks tailored to younger appetites.

Online Sandwich Queen's FAQs

1. How do I pay for my online orders

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and mobile payments (like Apple Pay and Google Pay) for online orders. Payment can be completed through our secure online checkout system.

2. Why I lost my login account

Accounts may be deactivated due to inactivity, security issues, or if the system detected multiple failed login attempts. Contact our support team to help you recover or reactivate your account.

3. What happens to my Facebook, Google & Wechat logn in?

You can continue using Facebook, Google, or WeChat to log in if you initially signed up with these platforms. If you encounter issues, please reach out to our customer support.

4. Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes, we offer both takeout and delivery options. You can place an order through our website or app for your convenience.

5. How can I contact the delivery driver?

Once your order is on the way, you'll receive a tracking link where you can view the delivery status. For any issues, please contact our customer service team.

6. What is your cancellation policy?

Orders can only be canceled within a few minutes of placing them. After that, our team begins preparing your food. For further assistance, please contact customer support.

7. Do you have online gift cards available?

Yes, we offer online gift cards that can be purchased on our website and sent directly to recipients by email. They are redeemable both in-store and online.
